# TD-Bank-Campaign-Analysis
Optimizing Term Deposit marketing campaigns using Power BI. A case study in increasing conversion rates through targeted segmentation and operational efficiency.

## 📊 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes marketing campaign data to identify high-potential customer segments and optimize operational efficiency. The goal was to increase Term Deposit subscriptions while reducing telemarketing costs.

## 🔍 Key Insights
1.  **Strategic "Stop Point":** Conversion probability drops below 10% after the **4th call attempt**. Recommendation to cap outreach at 4 calls to reduce operational waste.
2.  **Target Demographics:** **Students** (29%) and **Retirees** (23%) convert at 2x the rate of the general population.
3.  **Financial Friction:** "Dual Loan Holders" (Housing + Personal loans) have a significantly lower propensity to save compared to "Debt-Free" customers.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
* **Power BI:** Dashboarding, DAX (Dynamic Measures), Power Query (M Code).
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema, Custom Binning (Duration/Age).
* **Statistical Analysis:** Frequency distribution & diminishing returns modeling.
